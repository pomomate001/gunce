## English (en-US)

**App name**
Günce: Diary & Mood Journal

**Short description**
A private, beautiful diary with mood tracking, photos, voice notes and stickers.

**Full description**

Günce turns your days into beautiful pages. Log your mood with one tap, add photos and voice notes, and decorate pages with stickers. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no server.

✨ A BEAUTIFUL PAGE FOR EVERY DAY
• Title, text, photos and voice notes on one page
• 85+ stickers you can drag, resize and rotate
• Paper backgrounds: lined, dotted, cream, night and more
• 12 fonts and ink colours

😊 MOOD TRACKING
• 8 moods, one tap
• Cute emoji packs: Mochi, Teddy, Bunny, Puppy, Cats and Weather
• A fresh writing prompt every day

📊 UNDERSTAND YOURSELF
• Daily streak and longest streak
• Mood breakdown and monthly mood flow
• Year in pixels: each square a day, each colour a feeling
• See every day's mood on the calendar

🎨 ANIMATED THEMES
Dawn, Ocean, Forest, Midnight, Aurora, Sakura and more, each with a gently moving hand-crafted landscape.

🔒 PRIVATE AND SAFE
• PIN and fingerprint lock
• Your diary stays on your device and never reaches us
• One-file backup and restore

⏰ GENTLE REMINDER
A daily nudge at the time you choose, with a different message every day.

👑 GÜNCE PREMIUM
Every theme, emoji pack, sticker, font and paper. No ads.
